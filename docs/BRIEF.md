# Brief — fuzzynop.com v2

## Goal
A quiet home for Josh Schwartz / FuzzyNop. Hacker and former executive who returned to art, community, and the questions underneath it all. Now working at the intersection of consciousness, wellness for hackers, AI safety, and cyborg psychology.

## Core question
What happens when humans and machines meet, and how do we stay human in the process?

## Audience
People who already sense who he is — collaborators, peers, the curious. The site confirms and invites; it doesn't sell.

## Aesthetic
- Dark cyborg. Elegant machine × human futurism. Organic + machine.
- High-contrast black, white, and gray. Traffic-cone orange only as the printer's hot tip — never a block, never behind text.
- Material 3 structure (roles, type scale, shape, motion) — re-skinned, not Google-looking.
- Mood: Limbo (fog, paper-cut ink silhouettes) × machine arms constructing the page (Westworld titles), spider-like biomech, hexagons, ink splatter. A small ink character of Z gets carried around by the arms.
- Layout reference: shaqdeff/Portfolio-Template — single page, motion on scroll; ours is an offset stage of printed plates, not stacked bands.

## Tone
Understated. No hype, no buzzwords, no "passionate about" lists, no name-dropping. Let the work speak. Dry wit at most once (`0x90` footer).

## Success criteria
- Feels calm and considered; reads in under a minute.
- Every claim sourced (`docs/CONTENT.md`).
- Lighthouse ≥ 95, WCAG AA, works at 375px, reduced-motion safe.
- Deployed on Vercel at fuzzynop.com (apex) + www redirect, preview deploys per branch.

## Out of scope (v2.0)
Blog/CMS, contact form backend, i18n, e-commerce. (Candidates for v2.x.)
