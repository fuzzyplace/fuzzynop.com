De-rolling for Hackers

Taking the role off on purpose

Actors have a name for stepping out of a character. This talk asks what red teamers can borrow, so the person doing the work stays part of the system.

They spend many, many years learning how to get into role, but they're never taught techniques about getting out of it.

Sally Bailey, professor and director of Kansas State University's drama therapy program. ScienceDaily, June 6, 2014 (opens in a new tab).

HEACKERz, Hackers Healing Hackers, is a community. The talk sits with that idea: safety includes the people doing the work.
